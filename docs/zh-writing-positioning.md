# 中文写作线定位 (Chinese Line Positioning)
Locked 2026-09-23 by Ang. Governs the 「中文写作」 chat (60c0d0ac). Sibling of `docs/substack-constitution.md` (English line) — the two lines are separate rooms: EN = serious, Mira's first-person perspective; ZH = casual, human's perspective.

Supersedes `docs/north-star.md` §6 中文支线 (米拉的页边小记) — that plan is retired.

## Voice
- Human's voice: Ang's. Relaxed, casual, 大白话 — like talking to a smart friend over tea. Never stiff, never lecture-y.
- Not Mira first-person. On this line Mira is helper and editor (research, structure, drafting support) — but the voice on the page is Ang's. This is the mirror image of the English line, where Mira holds the pen and writes her own thoughts.
- Language: 简体中文.

## Platform
- 主阵地：播客 — Apple Podcasts + RSS 全网分发，月更 1–2 期对谈。(播客名 + 确切节奏待定。)
- 文字存档：Matters / 独立博客 — 抗审查备份，不指望增长。
- 分发：小红书 — 只做分发，600–800 字图文卡片引流；不做长文。
- YouTube = 播客视频版：对谈固定机位顺手录，音频走 RSS、视频上 YouTube，不单独做 YouTube 内容。
- 微信公众号：已排除 (Ang 不喜欢)。
- Background: platform research 2026-09-23 (`chinese-writing-platforms-20260923-1716`); comparison page artifact 「中文写作平台对比」.

## Content & pipeline
- 中文长文章讨论区。选题从日常讨论里顺手提，不用定时任务催。(e.g. 《两种中国：干与湿》灵山/理埠 — 中文线，不进 Substack 池。)
- Pipeline: 讨论 → seed (track=zh) → 草稿回来 signoff → 发布。Ang 批准一切，publication gate 与英文线相同。

## Values
- No slop, reader is smart, low volume high signal — same bar as the English line.
- Casual ≠ sloppy: relaxed tone, but every piece still earns its place.
- Scope: 中文长内容。英文 AI/技术归 Substack 那边，两边不串味。
